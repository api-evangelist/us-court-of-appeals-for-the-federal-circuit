# US Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (us-court-of-appeals-for-the-federal-circuit)
The US Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit is a federal appellate court with nationwide jurisdiction over cases involving patent law, international trade, government contracts, federal employment, veterans' benefits, and other specialized areas of federal law.
